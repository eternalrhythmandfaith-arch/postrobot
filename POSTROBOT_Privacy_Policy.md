# Privacy Policy — POSTROBOT

**Last Updated:** July 29, 2026

**POSTROBOT** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you connect your accounts and use our services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect
When you authorize POSTROBOT to connect to your social media accounts (including TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram), we may collect the following limited information:
- **Account Identifiers:** Basic profile information such as your User ID, username, and profile display name provided via official OAuth 2.0 authorization.
- **Authentication Tokens:** OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens required to interact with third-party APIs on your behalf.
- **Content Data:** Metadata related to the videos, images, and captions you schedule for publication.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information exclusively for the following purposes:
- To authenticate your account and execute requested video uploads and publications.
- To verify publication status and maintain scheduling logs.
- To display account connectivity status within your local management interface.

We **DO NOT** sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information or API tokens with any third parties or advertisers.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
- All authentication tokens and configuration settings are stored locally on your desktop environment within encrypted environment files.
- We implement industry-standard technical measures to prevent unauthorized access to your authentication data.

## 4. Third-Party API Data Handling
POSTROBOT utilizes official third-party APIs (including TikTok Content Posting API and Meta Graph API). Our handling of data retrieved from these APIs complies strictly with:
- TikTok Developer Terms and Privacy Policy
- Meta Platform Terms and Data Use Checkup

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion
- Authentication tokens are retained only for as long as necessary to maintain active account connectivity.
- You can revoke POSTROBOT's access to your TikTok or social media account at any time through your account security settings on TikTok or the respective platform.
- Revoking access immediately invalidates all stored tokens.

## 6. Children's Privacy
POSTROBOT is not directed to individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
